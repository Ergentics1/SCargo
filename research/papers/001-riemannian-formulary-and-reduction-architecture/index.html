Why Compression Begins With Comprehension

The November 2026 machine-verified formalization of the Riemann Hypothesis and Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions checks five headline theorems from only the three standard foundations of classical mathematics. Yet at more than fifteen million lines across nearly 150,000 files, its physical mass creates a steep barrier to mathematical reading, independent auditing, and long-term library integration.

Rather than running speculative global edits directly on a verified proof, this clean-room study separates historical comprehension (tracing every load-bearing pillar across five mathematical eras), lexical consistency (a unified vocabulary bridging number theory and proof engineering), independent measurement, and stratified controlled trials gated by five non-negotiable safety invariants.

Five-Era Reading Arc
Era I (1837–1896): Dirichlet characters, Riemann symmetry, and classical zero-free regions.
Era II (1946–1952): Adelic zeta integrals, Guinand–Weil positivity, and Behrend sets.
Era III (1964–1989): Stepanov multiplicity emulated via Weil theta lifts & local representation theory.
Era IV (1974–2012): Regularized Siegel–Weil periods, Galois compatibility, and Arakelov slopes.
Era V (2022–2026): Prismatic integral lattices and the complete machine-verified synthesis.

Stage 2 · Stratified Controlled Trials

Pre-Registered Trial Design & Five Safety Invariants

Each candidate compression rule is evaluated on balanced, randomly allocated Treatment and Control module pairs stratified by mathematical domain and file size (initial trial: 162 balanced forwarding-wrapper modules). A rule qualifies only if it achieves net reduction while passing all five invariants:

  1. Import Integrity: Zero missing or dangling module references.
  2. Strict Acyclicity: Zero cycles anywhere in the dependency graph.
  3. Zero Core Drift: Protected theorem statements and toolchain configuration remain untouched.
  4. Full Local Compilation: Every edited module and every direct downstream importer builds cleanly.
  5. Axiom Preservation: Only classical foundations (propositional extensionality, quotient soundness, choice); zero unproven placeholders.
